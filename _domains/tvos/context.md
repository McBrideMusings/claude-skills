# tvos — injected context

> No touch, no cursor. The focus engine moves selection. Design for distance.

Stacks with [../apple/](../apple/) and [../gui/](../gui/). Everything here is a difference
from a phone or a desktop, not a repeat of them.

- **Focus is the whole interaction model.** The remote moves focus between elements; there is
  no pointer and nothing arbitrary is reachable. An element that cannot take focus cannot be
  used at all, and focus order is a design decision, not a side effect of layout.
- **Read at three metres.** Type sizes, hit targets and contrast that work on a laptop are
  unreadable across a room.
- **Text entry on a remote is painful.** Minimise sign-in and typing.
- **Overscan is real on some displays.** Keep content inside the safe area.
